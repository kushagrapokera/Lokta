# UI questions — exact on-screen text, in flow order

## Must screens (everyone)

1. What do you need the loan for?
   - Purpose
   - Which one fits best?
   - Or describe (only if Purpose = Other)

2. How much do you want? (Rs.)
   - Amount (Rs.)

3. In how many years do you want to repay?
   - Years

4. What do you do?
   - Occupation
   - How do you get paid? (Fixed salary in bank / Own shop/business, file ITR / Daily/weekly/gig cash)

5. Your net monthly in-hand income? (Rs.)
   - Yours (salaried / self-employed) or LOW month income + HIGH month income (informal)
   - Does anyone else earn in family? (No / Yes)
   - Their monthly income
   - Is that income steady right now? (Steady / Stopped)

6. Total EMI + app-loan + BNPL you pay per month? (Rs.)
   - Any EMI bounced in last 12 months? (no / yes / unknown)
   - How many bounces?

7. Total household expenses per month incl rent? (Rs.)
   - Confirm screen when left blank: We assumed Rs.X monthly spending. Correct?

8. Your age?
   - Your age?

9. Credit score?
   - Type your score
   - Confirm screen: We read this as: X. Correct?

10. If income stops for 2 months, how will you pay EMI?
    - Backup (No backup / Family/friend covers 1-2 EMIs / Savings 1-2 months / Savings 3+ months)

11. Have you already got a loan offer?
    - Skip / Yes
    - Offer rate %
    - Processing fee %
    - Offer tenure (months)

## Branch screens (all skippable)

Salaried only:
- How long in your current job? (<1yr / 1-3yr / 5yr+)
- Employer type? (Small company / Other / MNC / Govt / Large co.)
- Card use (No card / <30% / 30-70% / >70%)

Self-employed only:
- ITR annual
- Its value in Rs. (0 if none) + Loan-free? (No / Yes) + Property type? (residential / commercial)
- Business vintage (<2yr / 2-10yr / 10yr+)
- Extra per month (0 if none) — business extra income

Informal only:
- Extra per month (0 if none) — vehicle extra income
- Outstanding (0 if none) + Rate % — app loans detail
